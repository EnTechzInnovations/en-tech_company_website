About EN-TECHZ Innovations

Empowering Careers & Businesses Through Technology

EN-TECHZ INNOVATIONS is a professional IT training and web development company in Thrissur, Kerala. We offer structured bootcamps, internship programs, and academic project support alongside website development for shops, startups, and growing businesses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our programs and services at ENTechz Innovations, Thrissur.

We run short-term intensive bootcamps in four domains: Python Full Stack, MERN Stack Development, AI & Machine Learning, and Flutter App Development. Programs run from 3 days to 15 days depending on depth. Every session is instructor-led with live coding, hands-on assignments, and real-time demonstrations — not recorded videos, not slides only.

Internship programs are available in: Python & Django, MERN Stack, MEAN Stack, Frontend Development (React), Flutter, AI & Machine Learning, Data Science, Data Analytics, and Database Management (SQL / MongoDB). If you're unsure which domain fits your goals, reach out and we'll help you figure it out.

Internships run for 1 month, 3 months, or 6 months. The structure is task-based — you work on real projects, go through regular mentor evaluations, and build a portfolio by the time you finish. It's structured like an actual company environment, not just another course.

Academic project support covers everything from start to submission: topic selection based on current technology trends, full development (design, coding, testing), documentation, and viva voce and presentation preparation. The goal is that you genuinely understand your project — not just submit it.

Bootcamps are short and intensive (3–15 days) — good for learning a specific technology quickly or getting a strong foundation before something bigger. Internships are longer (1–6 months) and simulate a real work environment with ongoing projects, task deadlines, mentor feedback, and portfolio output. If you want job-readiness, the internship is the better track.
